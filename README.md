# Scam-Alert
Today, many people in India are losing money because of online scams through WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS, and phone calls. Scammers use fear and urgency to trick people. For example, they may threaten people with digital arrest or say their bank account will be blocked.

Existing tools have some limitations. Truecaller mainly identifies phone numbers, and Google Messages focuses on SMS. Government websites help people report scams, but they don't provide the type of instant message analysis we are building.

So, our main idea is to detect scams by understanding not only suspicious links but also the meaning and intention behind a message.
